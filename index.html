What happens next

What happens when you submit

Your proposal goes straight to the Chair, Dr. Pacheco, who confirms within 5 business days that it is complete and schedules the review, or contacts you with questions. You receive a copy by e-mail.

Reviewers assigned to the category weigh the evidence against what DCG uses now. Voting members decide on scientific and educational merit only.

You and the Instrumentation Committee receive the written recommendation within 10 business days of the vote.

From submission to decision

  1. Day 0. You submit this form with your documents.
  2. Within 5 business days. The Chair confirms completeness, or returns it with guidance.
  3. Before the meeting. Reviewers weigh the evidence against what DCG uses now.
  4. At the meeting. Voting members decide on merit only; requestors present and leave before the vote.
  5. Within 10 business days. You and the Instrumentation Committee receive the recommendation.
